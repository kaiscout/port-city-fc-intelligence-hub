# MoSCoW Prioritization

## Must

- Authentication, six roles, server authorization, secure sessions, and audit.
- Onboarding, organization/user/settings management, named-domain CRUD.
- Validated manual and CSV inputs, templates, preview, duplicate control, atomic commit.
- Executive, scenario, funnel, sponsor, program, community, report, and case-study sections.
- Transparent calculations, provenance, deterministic demo, clean organization.
- PostgreSQL constraints/migrations, backup/restore, health/logging.
- Responsive, accessible, print-capable experience and meaningful tests.

## Should

- Filter persistence, saved report presets, richer import reconciliation, approval status on calculation assumptions, and scheduled audit review reminders.

## Could

- Approved read-only provider connectors, SSO, row-level organizational partitions beyond the current organization boundary, signed report bundles, and scheduled data-quality summaries.

## Won’t in this pilot

- Ticket issuance, accounting ledger, payments, outbound email, live social posting, public registration, or claims of formal certification. These exclusions protect focus and avoid false integration claims.

## Decision rationale

Must items prove the end-to-end operating model and controls. Should items improve usability after real staff behavior is observed. Could items depend on provider/access discovery. Won’t items belong to systems of record or materially expand risk.
