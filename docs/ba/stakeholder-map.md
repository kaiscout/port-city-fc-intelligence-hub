# Stakeholder Map

| Stakeholder                 | Interest | Influence | Primary need                                 | Engagement                              |
| --------------------------- | -------: | --------: | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Board / leadership          |     High |      High | Decision narrative, risk, investment case    | Monthly decision review                 |
| Club administrator          |     High |      High | Configuration, access, audit, recovery       | Product owner; weekly pilot triage      |
| Analyst                     |     High |    Medium | Definitions, comparable data, scenarios      | Working sessions; owns methodology      |
| Commercial lead             |     High |      High | Fulfillment evidence and renewal actions     | Weekly account review                   |
| Marketing lead              |     High |    Medium | Funnel, cohort, and campaign efficiency      | Fixture campaign review                 |
| Matchday operations         |     High |    Medium | Fixtures, readiness, attendance, costs       | Pre/post-fixture workflow               |
| Community lead / partners   |   Medium |    Medium | Evidence definitions and reporting           | Monthly evidence review                 |
| Finance/accounting owner    |   Medium |      High | Approved aggregates and reconciliation       | Close-period sign-off                   |
| Ticketing / provider owners |   Medium |    Medium | Stable import boundary                       | Integration discovery only              |
| Supporters and participants |     High |       Low | Privacy, accessibility, respectful reporting | Indirect; privacy and feedback controls |

## Engagement principle

High-influence stakeholders approve definitions and gates; operational users co-design input effort and exception handling. External stakeholders are not represented as having participated in this independent case study.
