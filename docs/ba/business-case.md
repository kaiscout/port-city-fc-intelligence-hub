# Business Case and Problem Statement

## Decision context

Port City FC is growing men’s and women’s pre-professional programs in Wilmington while operating as a community-centered club. Official USL announcements support the facts that the men joined USL League Two and the women joined the USL W League for 2026, alongside the club’s public emphasis on pathways, youth relationships, nonprofits, and ticket-linked charitable support. No commercial figures in this case study are asserted as club results.

## Problem statement — BR-001

Leadership needs to answer: **How can Port City FC grow both programs while improving matchday profitability, sponsor value, fan retention, operational readiness, and community impact?** The likely source records are distributed across ticketing, accounting, web analytics, campaign tools, sponsor working files, and operational notes. That fragmentation creates delayed metrics, inconsistent definitions, opaque assumptions, and avoidable manual reporting.

## Proposed response

Create a decision-support and operational-intelligence layer that accepts controlled manual/CSV inputs, applies transparent rules, protects role boundaries, and produces decision-ready dashboards, scenarios, and exports. The Hub does not replace payment, ticketing, accounting, email, analytics, or identity systems.

## Value hypothesis

- Better break-even and downside visibility improves fixture planning.
- Funnel evidence directs spend toward conversion and retention.
- Fulfillment controls protect sponsor value and renewal timing.
- Comparable program measures support equitable investment.
- Explicit provenance protects the credibility of community reporting.

## Investment logic

The pilot uses open-source components and a reproducible PostgreSQL setup with no paid dependency. A 90-day parallel run should prove decision quality, data effort, user adoption, and control effectiveness before provider integrations or production hosting are approved.

## Approval gates

Stakeholder validation, real-data reconciliation, security review, privacy/retention decisions, hosting approval, and user acceptance are mandatory before unsupervised organizational use.
