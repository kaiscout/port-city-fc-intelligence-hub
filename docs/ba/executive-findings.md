# Executive findings and proposed decisions

Presentation candidate: 6.0, 18 October 2026. All findings below describe synthetic/generalized public data. They do not establish PCFC's actual financial or operating position.

## Findings supported by the demonstration

1. **Cash movement and cash balance are different decisions.** FY25 synthetic inflow is $67,500 and outflow $63,700, leaving $3,800 of net movement and a 5.63% net cash margin. An opening balance and payment schedule are not supplied; cash runway and reserve sufficiency are unknown.
2. **Income concentration is visible, but contract value needs a separate scope.** Sponsorship is $38,000, or 56.30% of FY25 synthetic cash inflow. The independent 2026 account sample has $139,000 contract face value. Neither measure can be substituted for the other.
3. **Delivery exceptions can be assigned to owners.** In the 2026 synthetic sponsor sample, 43 of 54 contracted assets are delivered and three assets are overdue across two accounts. This illustrates a review workflow, not a finding about actual partners.
4. **Fixture measures need equivalent populations and denominators.** The eight 2026 synthetic home-fixture examples have 11,839 total paid attendees and $120,050 direct contribution before shared overhead. Program comparisons use per-fixture measures, aggregate capacity fill and paid-weighted returning share. These examples do not establish full-season opportunity or relative program investment value.
5. **Acquisition volumes reconcile within their own sample.** The synthetic club-wide channels sum to 28,400 visits and 3,214 purchases. The 11.32% visit-to-purchase ratio demonstrates the calculation; it does not prove an actual conversion bottleneck, causal campaign effect or tracked user cohort.
6. **Arithmetic assurance has a defined boundary.** Hub controls and the source-model manifest are calculated and exported. Source authenticity, current club records, actual data freshness and stakeholder acceptance require independent validation.

## Decisions requested for a controlled pilot

- Agree a finance data owner, reporting cutoff, KPI definitions and a process to propose reserve policy from authorized balance/payment data.
- Confirm actual sponsor accounts, contract dates and receipt treatment; nominate accountable delivery owners and an exception-review cadence.
- Confirm the home/away schedule, paid-versus-scanned attendance definition, direct-cost basis and allocation of season commitments before building a forecast.

## Hypotheses to test, not established findings

Retention, ticket-start conversion, sponsor activation and equivalent program investment may offer opportunities. Test them with approved data and explicit outcome measures before recommending spending or reallocating resources. The synthetic example does not justify funding a particular program or campaign.
