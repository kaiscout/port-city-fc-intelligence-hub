# Scope and Exclusions

## In scope

- Executive KPIs, global filters, trends, risks, and recommendations.
- Matchday scenario inputs, formulas, CRUD, comparison, and export.
- Attendance, funnel, channel, cohort, and campaign analysis.
- Sponsor profiles, contacts, packages, deliverables, reach, fulfillment, renewal risk, and print view.
- Equitable men’s/women’s program comparison and women’s business case.
- Community programs and impact records with provenance controls.
- Administrative CRUD for all named operational domains.
- Administrator-provisioned accounts, RBAC, secure sessions, audit events, and configuration.
- CSV templates, file/row validation, preview, duplicate protection, and transactional commit boundary.
- Demo/production-data modes, onboarding, health, logs, migrations, seed, clean organization, backup/restore guidance.
- Accessible tables, CSV exports, print views, methodology, BA case study, and documentation.

## Explicit exclusions

- Payment processing or cardholder data.
- Ticket issuance, seat inventory, refunds, or access control.
- General ledger, journal entries, tax accounting, or financial close.
- Bulk/customer email delivery or consent administration.
- Identity-provider replacement or self-service public registration.
- Live provider APIs until separately authorized, mapped, tested, and reconciled.
- Formal compliance certification or unsupervised production-readiness claim.
- Sporting performance, player health, scouting, or safeguarding records.

## Change control

Requests that add sensitive data, external writes, provider credentials, or public deployment require a new decision record, security/privacy review, ownership assignment, and acceptance criteria.
