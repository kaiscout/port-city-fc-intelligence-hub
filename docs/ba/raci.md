# RACI

Roles: Executive (EX), Administrator (AD), Analyst (AN), Commercial (CO), Marketing (MA), Matchday Operations (MO), Finance owner (FI), Community owner (CM).

| Activity                       | EX  | AD  | AN  | CO  | MA  | MO  | FI  | CM  |
| ------------------------------ | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Approve KPI definitions        | A   | C   | R   | C   | C   | C   | C   | C   |
| Configure organization/access  | I   | A/R | C   | I   | I   | I   | I   | I   |
| Maintain fixtures/attendance   | I   | A   | C   | I   | I   | R   | I   | I   |
| Reconcile revenue/cost imports | I   | A   | C   | I   | I   | R   | R   | I   |
| Create and compare scenarios   | C   | A   | R   | C   | C   | C   | C   | I   |
| Manage sponsor delivery        | I   | A   | C   | R   | C   | C   | I   | C   |
| Manage campaign/funnel data    | I   | A   | C   | I   | R   | C   | I   | I   |
| Record community impact        | I   | A   | C   | C   | C   | I   | I   | R   |
| Review audit history           | C   | A/R | I   | I   | I   | I   | I   | I   |
| Approve executive report       | A   | R   | C   | C   | C   | C   | C   | C   |
| Backup/restore verification    | I   | A/R | I   | I   | I   | I   | C   | I   |
| Incident coordination          | A   | R   | C   | C   | C   | C   | C   | C   |

R = Responsible, A = Accountable, C = Consulted, I = Informed. One accountable role is assigned for every activity.
