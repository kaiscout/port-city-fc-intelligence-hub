This is Port City FC Business Intelligence and Analytics: a business analysis portfolio project built to connect evidence, decisions and an accountable operating workflow. I designed the reporting model, source boundaries and user journeys, and developed the application with AI-assisted engineering. This tour shows the completed candidate, not a claim that the club has adopted it.

The starting problem was fragmented reporting: financial summaries, football operations assumptions, sponsor obligations and management questions lived in different places. A dashboard could look impressive while mixing incompatible numbers. I separated annual cash, fixture economics, contract face value and conditional planning. The public demonstration uses synthetic and deliberately generalized inputs. No private statements, staff contacts or attributed conversations are published. Reference dates describe the data, not the software build. The private application starts empty and reports only approved records entered into the signed-in organization.

In the public ownership view, the synthetic twenty twenty-five model has sixty-seven thousand five hundred dollars of inflow and sixty-three thousand seven hundred of outflow: a three thousand eight hundred dollar net cash change. That is not profit, bank balance or runway. Sponsorship represents roughly fifty-six percent of inflow, suggesting a concentration question for an owner to investigate, not a proven intervention or financial benefit. Fiscal and program filters respect their scopes, and the earlier year's unavailable monthly breakdown is left unavailable. Drill-downs and exports expose the evidence behind the selected view.

Scenario planning turns that question into a transparent conditional decision. Attendance is capacity capped, complimentary entrants do not buy admission, and ancillary receipts follow entered participation assumptions. Home-fixture count and sponsorship allocation frequency are explicit. Entered costs, community allocation and receipt allowances feed one equation, including break-even. Cases can be saved, compared and revised without erasing their history. In an independent test example, contribution is negative four dollars at thirty-six attendees and positive four dollars fifty at thirty-seven. That demonstrates a reproducible threshold, not a statistically validated forecast.

The private workflow connects the interface to PostgreSQL. Users configure records, enter or import data, review row-level errors, explicitly commit a valid batch, and see the resulting analytics. Corrections use versions; duplicate or changed-reference imports commit nothing. Settings, sponsor delivery, actions and user access are persisted. Server-side roles and organization scopes protect actual routes, and relevant writes append audit events in the same transaction. Verification exercises fresh sessions, restart persistence and an isolated recovery rehearsal. The companion workbook and Power BI sources explain the reconciliation, star schema, Power Query, DAX and program-role boundary; source validation is distinguished from native report acceptance.

The next step is controlled club validation: confirm source owners, approved actual inputs, accounting exclusions and user responsibilities, then agree operational hosting and acceptance. The value demonstrated here is a traceable decision process and working software, not invented savings, endorsement or achieved impact.
